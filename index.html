A neighbourhood hospital you can trust

Lakshmi Speciality Hospital, also known as Lakshmi Nursing Home, is a healthcare facility on S.S.V. Sala Street in Melur, Madurai district. We focus on maternity care and women's health, with general medical consultations for the whole family.

  • Committed to patient careEvery patient is listened to and treated with respect.
  • Experienced medical professionalsCare led by Dr. P. Amuthakalavalli, Gynecologist and Obstetrician.
  • Patient-friendly environmentA welcoming place for mothers and their families.
  • Quality healthcare servicesOpen day and night, so help is close when you need it.

Our Services

Care for women at every stage, and consultations for common health concerns.

Maternity Care

Care and guidance for expectant mothers, from check-ups during pregnancy to care after birth.

Gynecology

Consultation and treatment for women's reproductive health concerns.

Obstetrics

Medical care for pregnancy and childbirth, guided by an obstetrician.

Normal Delivery

Supportive care for mothers planning a normal delivery.

Women's Healthcare

Check-ups and advice for women's health at every age.

General Medical Consultation

Consultations for everyday illnesses and health questions.
